# We need a Community Lead

<p class="pr-meta">It's been a minute since our last post, and that's a problem.</p>

Hello readers! 
Quetoo [1.0.0 shipped in May](/news/quetoo-is-here). Since then:

 * 90 engine releases
 * ~1,500 commits
 * 3 mods
 * 4 maps
 * 5 selectable movement systems
 * A fully customizable HUD
 * In-game editor enhancements
 
The list goes on. And essentially nobody outside our Discord knows any of this happened 🫠

### Current social media presence

- **Discord** ([discord.gg/unb9U4b](https://discord.gg/unb9U4b)) — 400+ members, ~20 active on a given day
- **Facebook** ([Quetoo.org](https://facebook.com/Quetoo.org)) — 615 followers
- **YouTube** ([@WickedOldGames](https://youtube.com/@WickedOldGames)) — 155 subscribers; our most popular video, the one above, has [4,500 views](https://youtube.com/watch?v=GY2vPCj81bs)

The thing is: the people who find Quetoo tend to stick around. But practically nobody finds Quetoo 💀 We're building a fun and beautiful game, but failing to promote it and draw in players.

So we're looking for someone who can do that!

### The role

**Community and Marketing Lead.** 

You will own our blog and all of our socials. You'll be the voice of Quetoo.

- **Social media** posts, collages, clips, screenshot-of-the-week, whatever actually works on the platforms you know.
- **Video production:** trailers, feature spotlights, match highlights, devlogs. Whatever you're good at.
- **Community liason:** active in our Discord, welcoming new players, surfacing community content, etc.
- **Champion Quetoo** talk with content creators, map jammers, modders, and tournament organizers.

### Who you are

- **You love Quake.** You own Quake merch. You have a "Q" tattoo, or have considered one.
- **Extroverted.** This job is largely talking to people, in public, cheerfully.
- **Self-directed.** We need someone who can ship content and stay engaged without a lot of hand-holding. If you love to play Quake, take screenshots, record demos and turn them into videos, you probably have what we're looking for!

You don't need to be a programmer, a mapper, or a marketing professional. Taste and follow-through beat credentials here.

### What it pays

Nothing. This is a volunteer role on a volunteer project. We've never made a dime on Quetoo and we never will. In fact, it actually costs about $100/mo in hosting and infra to run this project. 🤑 This is a labor of love.

What we can offer is real: prominent credit in the game itself and on the website, a lead role and dedicated status in the Discord, full creative ownership of the voice and the channels, a portfolio's worth of published work with your name on it, as well as a seat at the table in steering the game.

### Interested?

Say hello on [Discord](https://discord.gg/unb9U4b). No portfolio is required — but if you've made something you're proud of, a video, a post, a community you've run, we'd love to see it.

Thanks!
