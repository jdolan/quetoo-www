# 🧟 A Slew of New Player Models

Tired of staring at the same soldier every match? Good news: Quetoo just got a lot more interesting to look at. **Eighteen new player models** are now available in-game, from grizzled marines to full-on nightmare fuel.

Say hello to Bloodseeker, Bunker, Caustic, Creech, Cruentus, Dragoon, Enforcer, Gammy, Gaunt, Gladiator, Gork, Guard, Mantis, Merc, Nitro, Sarge, and Violator — each with its own skin, animations, and personality. Some are battle-hardened grunts. Some are... not human anymore.
