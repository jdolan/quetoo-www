# 🧟 A Slew of New Player Models

Quetoo just got a lot more interesting to look at and listen to. **Twelve new player models** are now available in-game, sourced from the [ioQuake3](https://ioquake3.org/) and [DeFrag](https://ws.q3df.org/) communities, overhauled and upscaled for Quetoo.

Say hello to Bloodseeker, Caustic, Creech, Cruentus, Gammy, Gaunt, Gladiator, Mantis, Merc, Nitro, Sarge, and Violator. These are the cream of the crop for community-made Quake3 player models, resurrected with tender loving care and per-pixel lighting.
